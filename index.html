KrishiBot

A solar-powered robot that walks the field, checks each plant, and tells the farmer exactly which plants need spraying, in Hindi, Punjabi or English.

Try the demo scan

How it works

Farmers often spray a whole field when only a few plants are affected. KrishiBot finds those plants first.

  1. Powered by the sunA solar panel charges the battery that runs the robot.
  2. Walks every rowIt moves in a snake pattern so no plant is missed.
  3. Checks each plantA camera and image model name the problem: disease, pest or nutrient shortage.
  4. Advises the farmerA field map and short advice in the farmer’s own language.

Why it’s different

The technology is familiar. The decisions are what set KrishiBot apart.

Plants, not fields

It marks individual plants for treatment instead of advising a whole-field spray.

Knows when not to spray

A nitrogen shortage looks like disease. KrishiBot flags it as a fertiliser issue, so no pesticide is used.

Organic first

For pests like aphids it suggests a neem-based spray before any chemical.

Speaks the farmer’s language

Advice in English, Hindi and Punjabi, so it is usable without English.

One crop, one region

Focusing on wheat in Punjab allows training on real local field photos.

Safe by design

No doses are printed. Farmers are sent to their KVK to confirm the product and amount.
